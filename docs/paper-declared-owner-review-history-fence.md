# Disconnected declared owner-review history continuity fence

`inspect_declared_owner_review_history_fence(previous, current, now_ms)` performs a **read-only, caller-declared** comparison of two owner-review event snapshots. It rejects missing previous history, altered/deleted events, revision changes without append, appended events without a single revision advance, session/account changes, timestamp rollback, stale/future snapshots, duplicate approval IDs, duplicate approved client orders or intents, approval after rejection of the same client order ID or intent, rejection after approval of the same client order ID or intent, invalid approval lifetimes, expired approvals, and non-monotonic event recording. Input schemas are exact, populations are bounded to 512 events and review lifetime is capped at 30 seconds. The SHA-256 digest is a deterministic *display checksum*, not a signature or independent trust anchor.

A clean result is **`DECLARED_REVIEW_HISTORY_UNVERIFIED`**, never an approval. The snapshots, event IDs, clocks, digest and history are supplied by the caller, and can be forged together. Even a clean diagnostic always reports `history_authenticated=false`, `independent_audit_anchor_verified=false`, `owner_identity_authenticated=false`, `approval_signature_verified=false`, `approval_nonce_atomically_consumed=false`, `may_submit_paper_order=false`, and `may_submit_real_order=false`. A missing previous snapshot or inconsistent history returns `HOLD_DECLARED_REVIEW_HISTORY`. The module never contacts a broker, accesses secrets, stores approvals, reserves margin, or submits orders.

**Production prerequisites:** append-only server-side audit storage with independent authenticated checkpoints, verified per-order owner signatures and identity, transactionally consumed single-use approvals, fresh authenticated quotes/positions and margin, broker order/fill reconciliation, verified protective stops, and fail-closed reconnect/timeout handling. Owner must positively confirm **each specific real-money order**. Stops and risk previews cannot guarantee maximum realized loss or profitability. Do not merge/deploy without complete exact-head CI and independently verified backup restoration, rollback rehearsal, and authenticated production health.

A review `REJECT` is not a substitute for a broker cancellation or a cryptographically authenticated revocation of an earlier `APPROVE`. Conflicting terminal review decisions for the same client order or intent are quarantined; distinct unrelated reviews remain diagnostic-only. These checks operate on caller-declared data and cannot prove a review or its ordering occurred.


## Duplicate rejected reviews and identity continuity

The offline audit also holds on repeated `REJECT` decisions for the same client order ID or intent (`duplicate_rejected_order_or_intent`), client order IDs rebound to different intent digests (`client_order_rebound_to_different_intent`), intent digests rebound to different client order IDs (`order_intent_rebound_to_different_client_order`), and reuse of one preview digest across distinct order identities (`preview_digest_reused_for_distinct_order`). These are deliberately conservative diagnostic quarantines: a later review is **not** evidence that an order was cancelled, and a digest is not authenticated provenance. Distinct unrelated rejected reviews do not trigger these checks.

This does not replace a transactional broker-side idempotency key, an append-only trusted review log, authenticated revocation/cancellation receipts, or individually signed and atomically consumed owner consent. No approval, release, broker connection, paper order, or live order is permitted by this audit. Six synthetic regressions cover duplicate rejection, all three identity-binding failures, noninterference for unrelated rejections, and input immutability.


## Append-only checkpoint causality and atomic revision granularity (29 October 2026)

A newly appended review must have a declared `recorded_at_ms` **strictly later** than the prior snapshot's `observed_at_ms`. A record already timestamped at or before the earlier snapshot but absent from it is a retroactive backfill, not a proven new review, and triggers `appended_review_predates_prior_snapshot`. Each snapshot transition may append **exactly one** review event per sequence increment; appending multiple events in one revision triggers `multiple_reviews_appended_in_single_revision`, even when all timestamps are monotonic. These fail-closed checks prevent caller-declared batches and retroactive inserts from looking like an ordinary append. They do **not** authenticate timestamps, establish that an earlier snapshot was complete, or validate a durable independent audit checkpoint. A real provider or trusted audit-log backfill must be separately reconciled and must not be automatically released by this diagnostic. Six synthetic regression cases cover both time boundaries, legitimate forward progress, batch append, revision jumps and input immutability. No paper/live trading authority is granted.


## Revision-to-event population lower bound (29 October 2026)

A nonnegative review-history revision must be **at least** the number of recorded review events. A history declaring two events at revision one (or any event at revision zero) is internally impossible under the one-event-per-revision policy and now triggers `history_population_exceeds_revision`, including when the impossible count occurs in the **previous** checkpoint. An empty revision-zero history and a history with exactly one event per revision are consistent but remain **unverified**. Five synthetic regressions cover current/prior undercounts, equality, an empty genesis and input immutability.

This is only a necessary arithmetic consistency check. The starting revision may be higher than the event population; gaps, historical truncation, external log completeness and authenticity cannot be established from caller-supplied counters. A clean result still never permits paper or live execution, verifies a signature, or releases quarantined orders. Independent append-only audit anchoring and per-order positive owner consent remain mandatory.
