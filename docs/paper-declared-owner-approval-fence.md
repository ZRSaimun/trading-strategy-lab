# Disconnected owner-review fingerprint fence

`inspect_declared_owner_approval_fence` binds a **caller-declared** human-review record to the full order, quote, account, risk policy, recent order history, and computed offline order-risk result. It creates a deterministic SHA-256 *display fingerprint* for a review preview. The review record must match the client order ID, order intent digest, broker/account alias, quote source and observation timestamp, and full preview fingerprint. It must declare a positive decision after quote receipt, no later than the current evaluation time, with a lifetime of at most 30 seconds; it cannot reuse an ID in the caller-provided consumed-ID list. Missing, stale, mismatched, rejected or replayed declarations produce `HOLD_DECLARED_OWNER_REVIEW`. An underlying risk hold always propagates.

**This is not owner authentication or a broker execution gate.** The approval record, timestamps, consumed IDs and account/quote/order inputs are all caller-controlled. SHA-256 here is not a signature, HMAC, or verified proof of consent. Even a clean diagnostic returns `DECLARED_OWNER_REVIEW_UNVERIFIED` with `owner_identity_authenticated=false`, `approval_signature_verified=false`, `approval_nonce_atomically_consumed=false`, `broker_authenticated=false`, `may_submit_paper_order=false` and `may_submit_real_order=false`. No broker connection, credential handling, order submission, audit database or paid service is included.

For real-money readiness, implement an independently authenticated, single-use, **transactionally consumed** owner approval bound to a specific order and a server-side immutable risk snapshot. Verify signature and identity, quote freshness, holdings, account margin, current limits, stop acknowledgements and broker submission state atomically immediately before execution; require separate positive confirmation for **each** specific order. Treat network timeouts and unknown submission outcomes as quarantined until independently reconciled. Never infer CFD access from Trading 212's Invest/Stocks ISA API. Never claim a guaranteed stop fill, capped realized loss, or trading profitability. Do not merge or deploy this draft without full exact-head CI and independently verified production recovery/health gates.
