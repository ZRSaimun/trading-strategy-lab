# Loop 5 — declared-only chronological forecast probability-mix drift

`backend/app/forecast_probability_drift_audit.py` provides an **opt-in, disconnected, read-only** diagnostic for the distribution of issued probabilistic forecasts. It adds no feed, scheduler, notification, broker, database migration or order permission. The input is a `ForecastEventStore.load`-compatible snapshot, a caller-declared expected `(modelVersion, horizonCandles, baseStart)` schedule and explicit abstentions. None of these is independently authenticated.

The audit first invokes `audit_forecast_decision_coverage`, requiring **exactly one forecast or abstention for every expected completed candle**, no missing/unexpected/conflicting decisions, valid chronology and consistent source identity. Any failure withholds **all** distribution estimates; an empty schedule never produces a reassuring zero-divergence result. Per frozen model version and 1/3/12-candle horizon, the expected decision population is sorted by base candle start, split into first floor(n/2) and last ceil(n/2) decisions, and requires **at least 30 decisions and 30 issued forecasts in each half**. An abstention counts in the expected-decision denominator but never becomes a probability or a successful forecast. Insufficient cohorts are withheld.

Forecast probabilities (strictly between zero and one, validated by the existing scorecard) are assigned to five fixed, left-closed/right-open 0.2-wide bins, except the final upper edge is inclusive. The result reports exact bin counts, decision and forecast counts, abstention rates, chronological boundaries, and the **Jensen–Shannon divergence in natural-log nats** between early and late forecast distributions. Zero-count bins are handled without pseudocounts; divergence is symmetric and lies between 0 and ln(2). A strictly greater-than `warning_js_nats=0.10` descriptive threshold triggers operator review. Equality does not trigger a warning. Do not interpret this heuristic threshold as a calibrated statistical significance test.

This detects changing forecast *sharpness/composition* even before outcomes settle. It **does not measure calibration drift, accuracy, market regime, executable price, trading edge, statistical significance, paper P&L or maximum drawdown**. The forecast-only histogram can change because the abstention mechanism changed; the audit reports abstention rates for context, but does not eliminate that selection effect. Consecutive overlapping horizon outcomes are not independent. The source, clocks, journal, schedule and abstentions remain unauthenticated. Every output has `maySendNotification`, `mayExecutePaperOrder` and `mayExecuteLiveOrder` set to `false`.

Synthetic regressions: `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. pytest -q tests/test_forecast_probability_drift_audit.py`. Do not merge or deploy without exact-head CI, independent backup restore, rollback rehearsal and authenticated production health.
