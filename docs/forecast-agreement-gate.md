# Offline forecast agreement and abstention gate

`assessOfflineForecastAgreement` compares the **same-source, same-issued-candle** Jeffreys frequency and EWMA frequency estimates independently for 1, 3 and 12 completed-candle horizons. A horizon abstains if either model has insufficient samples, if the directional estimates conflict or differ beyond a fixed bound, or if neither estimate clears the configured margin from 50%. The output includes both original probabilities and their descriptive range; it does not average them or advertise calibration.

Defaults: maximum 15 percentage-point disagreement, minimum 5 percentage-point directional margin, maximum 60 seconds between caller-supplied issuance and assessment. Strict matching checks include source identity, base candle, issuance timestamp, horizon ordering, samples and observation close. Invalid evidence throws rather than producing a signal. No claim of independent models: both estimators reuse the same historical outcomes. Agreement is **not** evidence of accuracy, independence, profitability, statistical significance or calibrated probability.

This is **offline, disconnected, non-executable research only**. Caller timestamps are unauthenticated. `live=false`, `authenticated=false`, `persisted=false`, `alertsEnabled=false`, `execution=NONE` and `ordersSubmitted=0` are fixed. Do not wire the consensus labels directly to alerts or orders. No data source, broker, persistent outbox or actual demo fill is connected.

Run isolated synthetic regressions: `cd frontend && node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/forecast-agreement-gate.test.ts`.
