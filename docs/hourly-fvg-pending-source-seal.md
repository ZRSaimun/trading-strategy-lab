# Pending-hour source declaration seal regression

The causal 1H FVG as-of study must reject a pending (not yet closed) hourly candle whose timestamp accessor irreversibly changes the property descriptor of an already-declared source identity field. This includes dataset ID, archive SHA-256, instrument, provider, BID side, declared volume type, and the volume-verification flag. Even if the value is unchanged, the source declaration's representation changed *after* the completed prefix was read.

`frontend/tests/hourly-fvg-pending-source-seal.test.ts` supplies thirty-five synthetic cases (seven fields × four descriptor variants, plus seven deletion cases). Two variants permanently make one source field non-configurable, either retaining writability or also making it non-writable. A third variant retains configurability but removes writability during exactly one future timestamp read. A fourth variant makes the field non-enumerable while leaving it configurable and writable, hiding the declared identity from ordinary serialization. All four alter the snapshotted declaration without changing its value. Seven additional cases delete an own source field during the pending-hour timestamp read, without substituting an inherited value. These must fail closed as well, before any future market field is accessed. The scan must fail closed without reading any future OHLC, volume, or minute-coverage field. An irreversible mutation cannot be undone in place; callers must reconstruct the declaration from independent evidence before retrying. Even a technically reversible descriptor change must not be silently accepted as unchanged provenance.

These tests establish only in-memory declaration consistency. They do not verify a volume provider, license, raw source receipt, BID/ASK quote, execution cost, fill, trading outcome, or profitability. Keep the study draft, paper-only, unmerged and undeployed pending exact-head CI and independent evidence.
