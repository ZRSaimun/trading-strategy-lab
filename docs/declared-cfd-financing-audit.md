# Declared CFD financing completeness audit (offline, research-only)

`frontend/lib/declared-cfd-financing-audit.ts` checks a **caller-declared** set of financing rollover timestamps against **caller-supplied** statement-like cash-charge receipts for one exact provider, venue, instrument, account alias, position, settlement currency and statement SHA-256. It makes no network calls, does not connect to any broker, does not infer a CFD overnight schedule or charge rate, and does not route orders. The signed cash amount is positive for a debit and negative for a credit. Exact two-decimal fixed-point arithmetic avoids binary floating-point accumulation. Neither the archive hash nor a statement hash string authenticates the source.

A missing declared receipt returns `MISSING_DECLARED_EVENTS` and a **null** total, never a falsely complete or zero-cost estimate. An empty declared schedule returns `NO_DECLARED_EVENTS` with null total; it is **not proof that financing was free**. Only a one-to-one match of all declared rollover events yields `MATCHED_DECLARED`, still with coverage `SELF_DECLARED_SCHEDULE_AND_RECEIPTS_ONLY`. Unexpected, duplicate, cross-source, future or malformed receipts fail closed. Receipts may be recorded after the rollover but not after the declared audit as-of time. The code rejects unbounded schedules and extra outcome fields.

This is **not** a production CFD financing adapter, observed broker statement parser, full accounting ledger, P&L engine, fee forecast or proof of a profitable strategy. Genuine coverage requires independently obtaining the broker's instrument/account-specific rollover calendar (including weekend/holiday multipliers, DST/time zone, short/long direction, currency, financing changes, corrections and FX conversion), verifying all statement pages and broker signatures or retrieval provenance, and reconciling debits and credits against an authorized demo account. A complete *declared* schedule may still omit real events. Until then, all cost-adjusted backtests and holdouts must label financing **unverified** and must not claim realized returns or a trading edge. Never assume a Trading 212 CFD trading API exists.

Synthetic tests: `node --experimental-strip-types --test frontend/tests/declared-cfd-financing-audit.test.ts` (Node 22 from repo root). No licensed archives or credentials are used.
