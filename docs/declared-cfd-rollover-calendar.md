# Offline CFD rollover calendar coverage and DST preflight (Loop 3)

`backend/app/declared_cfd_rollover_calendar.py` adds a **read-only, disconnected** audit of a *caller-declared* broker/instrument/account/position-side daily CFD funding schedule. It is a narrow input-preflight companion to the existing `financing_vintage_audit` and `financing_position_population` modules. It does **not** infer actual broker rollovers or supply financing rates.

## Contract

`fingerprint_declared_rollover_calendar(scope, days)` validates a position-specific source identity, IANA timezone, local cutoff clock, UTC open/close/as-of/registration times and a declared source SHA-256. It requires **exactly one daily record for every local calendar date** from position open through close, including weekends and holidays. Each record explicitly states `CHARGE` (multiplier 1–7) or `NO_CHARGE` (multiplier zero and a reason), a UTC cutoff timestamp, IANA UTC offset and DST `fold`, published/ingested timestamps, and an evidence digest. It rejects missing/duplicate/reordered days, non-increasing UTC cutoffs, unbounded periods (>3,660 days), invalid UTC offsets, nonexistent spring-forward wall times, incorrectly declared fall-back folds, future cutoff/ingestion, impossible chronology and unknown fields. A multiplier of three is **never inferred** from Wednesday or any other weekday.

`audit_declared_rollover_calendar(scope, days, expected_calendar_sha256, expected_charge_timestamps)` additionally requires an exact expected fingerprint and an independently supplied (but still **caller-declared**) ordered population of *in-position* charge timestamps. A mismatch fails closed. It returns a self-declared calendar fingerprint, count and sum of declared charge multipliers, explicit due timestamps, late-evidence dates across **every daily calendar row** (including days before opening and after closing), and the number of distinct calendar years. It separately reports whether every *in-position due-charge declaration* was ingested by the **position-open timestamp**, plus a bounded list and full count of declarations unavailable at entry. The entry-time field is false when there are no due charges; it is never a proof of actual broker evidence or precommitted rates. `PRE_CUTOFF_DECLARATIONS_ONLY` means that declared daily evidence was ingested by each cutoff; `RETROSPECTIVE_DECLARATIONS_ONLY` means at least one record arrived later. Neither proves when a real broker published its calendar. `NO_DECLARED_DUE_ROLLOVERS_UNVERIFIED` never means free funding.

The daily census deliberately includes a **synthetic cutoff marker for no-charge days**, not a claim that a broker ran a rollover on those days. Real brokers may change cutoffs, suspend trading, charge more than once daily, or publish corrections. This version assumes a **single constant local cutoff per day** and rejects anything it cannot represent. Historical DST rules depend on the installed IANA timezone database. Source SHA-256 values and daily evidence digests are strings supplied by the caller, **not independently authenticated original broker files**; they can be regenerated after observing outcomes. The expected population can also be regenerated, so it is not a trustworthy external commitment.

The audit does not compute rates, overnight debits, FX settlement, interest, dividends, spread/slippage, P&L, statistical significance or untouched-holdout performance. It never makes a network request, uses a brokerage API, places a paper or real order, publishes licensed data, modifies the database, or enables execution. `may_execute_demo_order` and `may_execute_real_order` remain `false`.

## Verification and future integration

Run `PYTHONPATH=backend python -m pytest -q backend/tests/test_declared_cfd_rollover_calendar.py`. Synthetic tests cover UK fall-back and spring-forward DST, daily census and position-boundary gaps, wrong source/digests, late evidence even outside the position window, entry-time versus pre-cutoff evidence availability, no-due-charge safeguards, invalid multipliers, randomized declarations, and an eight-calendar-year NY-clock example. The tests **do not use authentic broker data**.

Next integrate a broker-native, authenticated **read-only** calendar/statement source, record the actual original-byte digest and retrieval time independently, model broker-specific calendar changes/holidays/rollover direction and real rates, and cross-check due timestamps with `financing_position_population`. Only then reconcile debits/credits and currency conversions against a complete demo account statement. Do not promote these declarations into actual execution cost measurements or a claim of profitable backtesting.
