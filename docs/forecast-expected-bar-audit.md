# Loop 5: expected completed-candle register reconciliation (offline)

`app.forecast_expected_bar_audit.audit_expected_completed_bars` is a pure, bounded, **read-only** cross-check between a caller-declared session-aware expected-bar register and the `ForecastEventStore.load()` journal view. It requires exact instrument/provider/venue/source/side/timeframe identity and checks that every expected candle is complete by the audit time, every journal CANDLE is on that register, and there are no missing, duplicate, extra, backward or premature receipts. Session breaks are permitted **only because the independent caller explicitly omitted closed-market starts**; the module never guesses a trading calendar or fills a gap. It never fetches market data, creates forecasts, sends alerts or executes trades.

This is a **structural** check, not proof that the caller's register was produced independently, that its market calendar is correct, or that a provider feed is authenticated/licensed. An exact match returns `DECLARED_MATCH_UNVERIFIED`, and all authentication, notification and paper/live execution permissions are false. It does not establish calibration, returns, broker BID/ASK, slippage or downside risk. An upstream trusted source must supply a complete externally retained expected-bar inventory and immutable receipts, including source timezone and market-session provenance; this function alone cannot enable live alerts or forward paper fills.
