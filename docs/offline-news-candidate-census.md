# Offline as-of macro-news candidate cohort census

`app.offline_news_candidate_census.audit_declared_news_candidate_census` provides a **disconnected, read-only** classification of at most 256 strictly increasing UTC-minute historical candidate decision times against one caller-declared economic-calendar source. It delegates each decision to the existing point-in-time event-collision auditor. Same-minute and future-observed revisions cannot affect the earlier decision, and even future malformed revisions fail validation. Input rows are never mutated.

Three mutually exclusive *declared-data* reasons are returned: `CONFOUNDED_DECLARED_WINDOWS` (overlapping or transitively touching windows), `DECLARED_SINGLE_WINDOW_UNVERIFIED` (one visible component, not proven causation), and `NO_DECLARED_WINDOW_UNVERIFIED` (missing calendar coverage may conceal releases). **Every candidate remains ineligible for verified event attribution or trade authorization**. Neither a zero declared-window count nor a single event proves source completeness, release provenance, market causation or a trading edge. Window widths are inclusive integer minutes from 0 to 1440; the same policy is used for every decision in the cohort.

Each row binds its as-of collision digest and full-visible-revision-history v2 digest. The cohort checksum binds source identity, decision sequence, policy and all rows, enabling deterministic research comparison without pretending the hash authenticates a vendor. Aggregate counts reconcile to the candidate population. No market data, broker integration, order execution, database migration, secrets, licensed archive or production route is involved. Worst-case repeated validation is bounded by 256 decisions and 5,000 revisions; use smaller batches for research.

Run `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. python -m pytest -q tests/test_offline_news_candidate_census.py`. Before evaluating historical gold, oil or Nasdaq news hypotheses, independently authenticate first publication/receipt clocks, calendar completeness, event units, source-local executable BID/ASK candles, financing, spread and slippage assumptions, and freeze candidate selection before untouched chronological holdouts. The module does not perform a backtest or estimate returns.
