# Development-only annual cost and downside fragility

`app.development_annual_cost_fragility.audit_development_annual_cost_fragility(manifest, trials, expected_manifest_sha256, adverse_multiplier="2")` is a **disconnected, caller-declared research diagnostic**. It does not open market archives, connect to a broker, submit orders, read validation/holdout results, or authenticate a preregistration.

The strict manifest declares one provider/venue/instrument/quote-side/UTC convention/account/currency/archive digest, a chronological development/validation/holdout boundary, a positive embargo, declared training/test label availability clocks, 2–32 candidates, and 3–128 ordered development folds covering at least three distinct UTC calendar years. Every candidate × fold requires exactly one bounded monetary receipt with gross P&L, spread, slippage, commission, signed CFD financing, FX charges, a source digest and an after-test/before-development-end receipt timestamp. Unknown fields, cross-source evidence, missing trials, duplicate receipts, premature outcomes or unavailable test labels, overlapping folds, invalid monetary strings and holdout outcome fields are rejected.

Baseline cash is gross less spread, slippage, commission, financing debits (or plus credits) and FX. Under an adverse-only 1–10× multiplier, nonnegative execution charges and financing debits rise, while financing credits are **removed**, not multiplied. The diagnostic reports per-candidate total baseline/adverse net, chronological maximum cash drawdown across folds, worst fold, annual net, a baseline development-only winner, and leave-one-year-out winners. It **does not** retroactively choose a strategy using validation or holdout data, and a winner changing under removal of one year is descriptive, not a statistical p-value.

The manifest fingerprint and post-outcome receipt digest are only integrity checks against *the caller's own declarations*. The label-availability clocks are also self-declared. They do not prove independent pre-outcome registration, source ownership/authenticity, full experiment disclosure, real bid/ask or market depth, CFD rollover calendars, broker financing/FX settlement, or a profitable trading edge. Even a positive declared net is not an executable performance result. Fold cash totals cannot reproduce intra-fold drawdowns, and different trading exposure or position sizing may make nominal cash comparisons misleading. Use a trusted immutable experiment registration, actual executable quote/statement evidence, normalized risk exposure, statistically defensible clustered uncertainty, and a genuinely untouched holdout before any performance claim.

Synthetic-only regression command: `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. python -m pytest -q tests/test_development_annual_cost_fragility.py`. No licensed archive bytes or credentials are committed. Demo and real order permissions are always false. Production merge/deploy require exact-head full CI and independent target ownership, backup restoration, rollback and health verification.
