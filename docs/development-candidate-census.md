# Loop 3 — offline development candidate-selection census

`backend/app/development_candidate_census.py` is a **disconnected, read-only** follow-up to `walkforward_fold_audit.py`. It reruns the underlying source-aware chronological fold validator from the original archives/observations/protocol/folds, rather than trusting a caller-provided audit result. It requires nonempty eligible training/test observation sets for every fold. No market data, P&L, broker records, accounts, secrets or order-sending interfaces are read or written.

A declared pre-test registration digest covers the exact development protocol, archive/source lineage, fold boundaries, eligible train/test observation IDs, exclusion counters and all candidate configuration digests. Every candidate must have **exactly one** bounded finite decimal score per eligible development fold, tied to the expected candidate configuration, source lineage and test-set digest. Duplicate/missing trials, extra undeclared candidates, post-development observations, premature selections, mismatched hashes, malformed scores, and inconsistent declared counts fail closed. Selection is deterministic: maximum *equal-fold mean of supplied scores*, with lexicographically smallest candidate ID winning ties. This is a bookkeeping policy, **not a recommended trading objective or statistically valid model-selection criterion**.

`candidate_registration_digest(...)` creates a deterministic **untrusted** hash to compare with an independent timestamped preregistration receipt. The module cannot prove that the receipt existed before testing, that no hidden candidates were tried, that scores are authentic, or that holdout outcomes were never viewed. A self-reported registration timestamp is not independent evidence. A complete *declared* census is not a complete *actual* experiment census. A validation/holdout result must never be fed into this module. Even an internally consistent result reports `DECLARED_ONLY`, `sourceAuthenticated=false`, `preregistrationExternallyVerified=false`, `candidateCompletenessExternallyVerified=false`, `profitabilityEstablished=false`, `mayExecutePaperOrder=false` and `mayExecuteLiveOrder=false`.

**Before any empirical claims:** independently verify licensed archive bytes and clocks, source/venue/quote-side identities, market gaps and survivorship; timestamp and store the preregistration outside the researcher-controlled dataset; log *all* tried candidates and hyperparameters; account for multiple comparisons and serial correlation; independently calibrate executable spread/slippage, commissions, CFD financing, FX, margin and risk; then evaluate untouched validation and holdout exactly once under the frozen policy. Never infer profitable expectancy from these synthetic regression tests.

Run synthetic tests with `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. pytest -q tests/test_development_candidate_census.py`. No API route, database migration, broker connection, execution permission, licensed archive publication or deployment is added. Production merge/release remains gated on exact-head CI, current-main reconciliation, independently verified backup restoration, rollback and post-deploy health checks.
