# Declared forecast direction-reversal audit (Loop 5)

`app.forecast_direction_reversal_audit.audit_forecast_direction_reversals` is an **offline, read-only, descriptive** diagnostic. It uses the existing replay-validated forecast/abstention decision census and never authenticates provider data, sends alerts, fills demo orders or authorizes live trading.

The caller supplies a `ForecastEventStore.load` snapshot, matching six-field source identity, an independently intended list of `(modelVersion, horizonCandles, baseStart)` decisions, declared abstentions, and an integer `as_of`. The shared decision-coverage validator rejects malformed inputs, future decisions, mismatched identities, duplicated or conflicting forecasts and abstentions. No metric is published unless the *entire* declared decision population matches. A matching self-declared schedule is still **unverified**.

For each model version and 1/3/12-candle horizon, chronological expected base starts are compared only in adjacent pairs exactly one declared candle duration apart. Missing calendar intervals, abstentions and neutral probabilities interrupt the sequence. Defaults: at least 60 expected decisions and 30 adjacent signed forecast pairs, a neutral band of `0.5 ± 0.05` (boundaries are neutral), and a strong reversal when both endpoint probabilities are at least 0.10 away from 0.5. A signed reversal occurs only across opposite sides of the neutral band. The diagnostic reports pair counts, reversal fraction, strong reversals, longest/trailing alternating run, and maximum adjacent signed probability jump. A reversal fraction >= 0.40 flags operator review. These are descriptive thresholds, **not statistical significance or a model defect**; rapid changes may be rational in a volatile regime. Inadequate support withholds rates.

All authority flags are hardcoded `false`. No outcome selection, settlement claim, trading edge, transaction costs, P&L, broker execution, recipient delivery or executable BID/ASK spread is inferred. The caller-declared schedule cannot establish session coverage, trusted server-owned UTC timestamps, licensed prospective completed-candle receipts or independently witnessed abstentions. Loop 1 must supply that evidence before any operational integration. Maintain draft/unmerged/undeployed status until full exact-head CI and independent production restore, rollback and authenticated health checks.
