# Source-isolated purged holdout plan (offline research-only)

`frontend/lib/purged-holdout-plan.ts` adds a **disconnected, metadata-only** partition planner for future multi-year evaluation. It is not wired to a live UI, broker, historical data importer, paper order engine or actual backtest. It does **not** read prices, outcome returns, positions, licensed archive bytes, or broker records. It cannot measure profits.

## Protocol

Before accessing holdout outcomes, separately preregister immutable `developmentEnd`, `validationEnd`, `holdoutEnd` (UTC epoch seconds), `embargoSeconds`, strategy rule version and SHA-256 of the frozen configuration. Maintain a trusted, append-only external record of the protocol, timestamps, data access and all candidate searches. This module does not provide such a record or enforce who can inspect the data. A SHA-256 string by itself is not source authentication, a time-stamped commitment or proof of an untouched holdout.

Pass explicit archive lineage entries with unique IDs, SHA-256 fingerprints, provider, instrument, venue, quote side, UTC convention and half-open coverage. The planner sorts entries chronologically, rejects overlaps and source-family drift, and reports coverage gaps without inventing candles. Different archive SHA-256 values are permitted only for explicitly declared, non-overlapping archives of the **same** source family. It does not prove market session coverage, source licensing or that archive bytes match their declared fingerprints. At least two elapsed development years are required; elapsed time is not proof of two complete years of market data.

Pass **timing metadata only**: unique observation ID, archive ID, first feature timestamp, decision timestamp and time when the label becomes available. Unknown fields are rejected to prevent realized outcomes from accidentally entering the planner. All three times must be chronological and inside the declared archive (label may equal exclusive archive end). Cross-archive feature/label windows are rejected rather than silently stitched; create independently verified, appropriately bounded archives before trying them. This deliberately sacrifices boundary observations rather than assuming warmup data can be reused without provenance.

Observations are assigned to chronological development, validation or holdout periods by **decision** time. An observation is excluded if its features begin before the period, if its outcome is available **at or after** the period's exclusive end (even with zero embargo), or if it becomes available after the end minus the preregistered embargo. For a positive embargo, a label available exactly at `periodEnd - embargoSeconds` is allowed. This strict segment-end check is necessary when a single archive spans multiple partitions: archive-exclusive-end purging alone does not prevent a validation label from arriving at the holdout start. The planner reports eligible IDs and exclusion reasons. It does not train, select hyperparameters, optimize the embargo, calculate returns, or certify an untouched holdout. Do not repeatedly alter the protocol after seeing validation or holdout performance. Avoid interpreting small sample counts as significant evidence.

## Verification and release

Synthetic tests: `cd frontend && node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/purged-holdout-plan.test.ts`. Coverage includes five-year archive lineage, disjoint partitions, boundary label purging, future-knowledge rejection, cross-period features, source-side drift, archive overlap, duplicate IDs, protocol failures, outcome-field rejection, source gaps, and later-data invariance. All sample data is invented test metadata; no observed market or broker results are included.

This module is a research primitive only. Before integration, independently verify archive digests from authorized raw data, label availability clocks, time zones/DST, quote sides, real spreads/slippage/commission/CFD financing, broker contract/margin specifications, train/test trade independence, frozen holdout access logs and prospective paper execution. Keep live-order routing disabled until eligible official provider/account support, fresh executable quotes, explicit owner-approved order details, risk caps and secure credentials are established.

No production migration, route, credential, network call, market-data archive, subscription, or real-money trade is involved. Do not merge or deploy until full latest-head CI and the exact Render backup, rollback and health-check plan are verified. `main` may auto-deploy.
