# Frozen baseline paired forecast-loss audit (Loop 5)

This offline, **disconnected** research helper compares one exact model/horizon's predeclared issuance-window forecasts with a single constant-probability benchmark frozen *before* the evaluation window. Call audit_frozen_baseline(snapshot, baseline, window_start=..., window_end=..., as_of=...) on a previously validated ForecastEventStore.load snapshot. The baseline requires baselineId, modelVersion, horizonCandles (1/3/12), baselineProbability strictly between zero and one, and frozenAt earlier than window_start. Window bounds and freeze are **self-declared**, not independently notarized or authenticated.

All matching issued forecasts count toward the population. If even one is pending, voided by a gap, settled after the declared as-of time, or immature at as-of, the whole comparison is WITHHELD_CENSORED and **all** loss/skill figures are null. Below 30 settled forecasts the result is INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE. Otherwise DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY reports paired mean squared probability error (Brier), frozen baseline Brier, 1 - modelBrier / baselineBrier, mean/worst paired excess loss, and maximum running cumulative excess loss in **squared-probability-loss units**. The latter is **not** trading drawdown. A positive skill value is **not** a profitability, significance, calibration, or forward-returns claim.

The tool enforces source identity, issuance/outcome linkage, exact horizon and model separation, strict status/probability checks, and the pre-window freeze timestamp. It cannot establish independent baseline preregistration, source authenticity, complete experiments outside the supplied journal, unmodified history, unbiased missingness, independence of overlapping horizon outcomes, statistical significance, executable spreads, fills, financing, or costs. Forecast-event replay and its database checks must run before this audit; this module is not a replacement for ForecastEventStore.load integrity checks. sourceAuthenticated, baselineFreezeExternallyVerified, statisticalSignificanceEstablished, forwardReturnsVerified, and all execution permissions are **false**. Returns and trading drawdown remain null. No worker, endpoint, notification transport, broker order, migration, dataset write, secret, or deployment is added.

**Release gate:** keep this offline research helper out of production until full exact-head CI, current-main review, legitimate prospective provider receipts and source entitlements, reproducible evaluation-window preregistration, and independently tested backup/rollback/health checks. Loop 1 must provide authenticated completed-candle receipts, session/gap policy and server-owned timestamps. Historical BID archives must never be relabeled executable quotes.
