# Offline development-only walk-forward fold audit (Loop 3)

`backend/app/walkforward_fold_audit.py` is a disconnected, metadata-only guard for chronological development folds. It takes **only** source-identification fields, declared observation timing, a frozen protocol, and train/test fold boundaries. It never accepts prices, labels, realized P&L, order records, credentials, broker accounts or market archives. It cannot run a strategy, validate execution costs or demonstrate profitability.

Each fold's training labels must be available **strictly before** `trainEnd`. Test labels must be available strictly before `testEnd` and no later than `testEnd - embargoSeconds`. Test features cannot reach behind `testStart`; training features cannot reach behind `trainStart`. Test intervals must be nonoverlapping, ordered and fully inside development, with a train-to-test embargo. Later test observations may be included in later training only after their labels are available. Validation and holdout intervals are excluded from all fold tests. Empty eligible train/test sets are explicitly insufficient.

All metadata is strictly schema-checked, bounded and source-identified: one declared provider/instrument/venue/quote side/UTC convention, distinct archive IDs and digests, nonoverlapping archive ranges, per-archive feature/decision/label clocks, and at least two elapsed years before the development cutoff. Archive gaps are counted, never filled. The SHA-256 strings and timestamps are *caller claims*, not authenticated archives or trusted preregistration. The development span does not establish continuous market coverage.

This is **not** a replacement for the existing `purged-holdout-plan` or independent holdout-access audit; it is an additional conservative fold gate for development-only experiments. An independently enforced, immutable preregistration and blind validation/holdout access controls are still needed. Reusing the same strategy selection process after inspecting validation/holdout outcomes contaminates those partitions regardless of this audit. Before performance research, independently verify archive rights, original bytes, source timebase, market sessions, data quality, actual bid/ask, contract multipliers, slippage, commissions, FX and CFD financing. Model-selection multiplicity and statistical uncertainty require separate analysis.

Synthetic test command: `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. pytest -q tests/test_walkforward_fold_audit.py`. Full backend/frontend/TypeScript/build/Docker/cloud/PostgreSQL CI and independent production backup restore, rollback and health checks are required before release. No runtime route, broker adapter, order submission, storage write or production deployment is added. `mayExecuteOrder` is always false.
