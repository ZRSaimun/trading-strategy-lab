# Offline two-cohort forecast calibration drift review

`frontend/lib/forecast-calibration-drift.ts` compares two **caller-declared**, equal-sized chronological blocks of settled non-overlapping 1-, 3- or 12-completed-candle forecasts. It delegates per-block Brier, log loss and five-bin expected calibration error to the existing `evaluateForwardReliability` validator. Cohorts must have exactly 50–250 forecast records each, the same model/version and exact source identity, fixed UTC phase, strictly contiguous slots including their boundary, and no duplicates. Missing records, voids, overdue pending outcomes, settlement later than the default 120-second tolerance (configurable 0–3600 seconds), future settlement and mixed sources prevent a scored report.

The report gives earlier/recent Brier and ECE plus signed differences. `CALIBRATION_AND_LOSS_REVIEW` requires Brier deterioration of at least 0.02 **and** ECE deterioration of at least 0.05. `LOSS_REVIEW` requires Brier deterioration of at least 0.02 and recent Brier above 0.25. Otherwise `NO_THRESHOLD_CROSSING` means **only** the arbitrary review thresholds were not crossed; it is not evidence of stationarity, calibration, statistical significance or trading profitability. Brier 0.25 is a fixed 50% forecast reference, not necessarily a strong climatology baseline. Thresholds were not optimized against holdout data.

**Safety and limitations:** All inputs, cohort declarations, receipt clocks, source IDs and outcomes are self-attested and unauthenticated. Equal-size non-overlapping outcomes do not eliminate serial dependence or selection bias; an external pre-registration/complete issuance ledger is necessary to prove no omitted bad forecasts. ECE has sampling noise and depends on binning. This is descriptive offline research (`UNVERIFIED_LOCAL_RESEARCH`, `live=false`, `authenticated=false`, `persisted=false`, `alertsEnabled=false`, `execution=NONE`), not a live drift alarm, notification transport, broker order preview or execution. No synthetic result is a real-market return.
