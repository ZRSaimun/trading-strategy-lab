# Hourly FVG first-touch midpoint rejection versus blind limit (research v6)

**Status: proposed, deterministic, read-only hypothesis.** This standalone TypeScript library is not yet wired to the terminal UI, production broker, archive query or cloud service. No actual BID/ASK executions, licensed news data, verified performance, live trades or profitability claims are included. It deliberately does **not** duplicate PR #24's sweep/MSS/FVG confluence risk-path experiment or PR #33's 4H CRT/15m confirmation screen.

**Version 6: known no-touch attrition.** When all 12 configured consecutive post-formation hours are observed and none intersects the midpoint or opens across it, `midpointTouch` and `strictCandidate` are both `FAIL`. Previously the strict-candidate field remained `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, incorrectly treating an observed non-candidate as missing evidence. A truncated/missing waiting window or opening gap-through remains `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`; no hypothetical fill or return is invented. The frozen rule identifier is `hourly-fvg-first-midpoint-touch-v6`. This changes cohort attrition labels, not observed prices or profitability. Synthetic bullish/bearish tests cover complete versus truncated horizons.

**Reproducibility:** Version 5 introduced exact wick arithmetic and retains v4's strict prior-20 volume arithmetic and compares the 50% rejection-wick threshold against exact integer representations of the supplied finite IEEE-754 OHLC prices. Subtraction can round a genuinely below-50% wick and its whole-bar range to an apparent equality. The reported wick fraction remains an approximate diagnostic; only the exact comparison determines the gate. This can change candidate eligibility at numeric boundaries, so earlier v1–v4 observations retain their original version labels. It does **not** certify volume provenance, source timestamps or market prices.

## Pre-registered definition

The engine `frontend/lib/hourly-fvg-midpoint-study.ts` accepts **one** immutable archive identity (64-character dataset ID and archive SHA-256), provider, exact instrument, BID price side, optional verified exchange/tick volume provenance, and a strictly ordered, positive-price sequence of fully observed **UTC-aligned 1H candles** (60 observed minutes each). Gaps remain gaps; it never invents candles or joins providers.

- **Formation**: three consecutive completed hourly bars `A, B, C`. Bullish gap when `C.low > A.high`; bearish when `C.high < A.low`. The gap and its 50% midpoint become known **only after C closes**.
- **First-touch freeze**: search at most the next 12 consecutive hourly bars for the **first** bar whose full high/low spans the midpoint. No later touch can replace a failed or unobservable first touch. Missing bars before that touch are `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`; a fully observed 12-hour window without a touch is `FAIL` for both midpoint touch and strict-candidate eligibility (v6). An opening gap through the midpoint is **not** treated as a known limit fill.
- **No-touch opening gap-through**: a post-formation bar opening on the *far side* of the midpoint makes the original hypothetical blind limit fill unobservable even if that bar's high/low never includes the midpoint. Freeze this event as `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` immediately; do not select a later midpoint return as the first touch. `firstTouchClosedAt` remains null if no actual midpoint-range intersection occurred. This rule applies symmetrically to bullish and bearish gaps. If that gap-through bar also spans the midpoint intrabar, record its physical first-touch close timestamp, but still classify the hypothetical limit fill as unobservable.
- **50% rejection wick**: on that first midpoint-touch bar, bullish lower wick = `min(open,close)-low`, bearish upper wick = `high-max(open,close)`. Wick / whole bar range must be **>= 0.50**, and the completed bar must close back beyond the midpoint with a directional candle body. This is a candidate research threshold, not a proven improvement.
- **Exact rejection threshold (v5)**: calculate the lower/upper wick and full range from exact binary representations of the supplied finite OHLC endpoints before comparing with the configured wick fraction. A rounded descriptive `rejectionWickFraction` of `0.5` does **not** imply the exact 50% gate passes. A truly equal 50% wick remains eligible when reclaim and other evidence gates pass. Synthetic extreme-price fixtures test arithmetic only; they are not real quotes.
- **Numerical volume threshold**: the 1.5× (or frozen configured) comparison uses exact binary representations of the provided finite numbers; no float sum or ratio is used to decide a strict boundary. A synthetic 1e100 baseline and 1.5000000000000001e100 touch now correctly qualify, while an exact 100/150 tie does not. This is numeric consistency, not evidence that a caller's `volumeVerified` flag authenticates its provider.
- **Volume gate**: only an externally verified exchange/tick classification plus **20 immediately preceding contiguous 1H bars with positive recorded volume** can qualify; the touch-bar volume must exceed **1.5×** their mean. Missing/zero/unverified volume is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, not PASS. A below-threshold, positively observed value is FAIL. Passing a synthetic `volumeVerified=true` test fixture is **not** source authentication; production must separately verify volume provenance.
- **Volume numeric integrity**: the prior-20 mean and current-bar comparison use exact binary integer mantissas/exponents of the provided finite values, rather than summing raw floats or dividing by a scale. This prevents large finite observations from overflowing to `Infinity`, very small positive observations from being rounded to zero, and rounded ties at the strict `> 1.5×` comparison. This is a deterministic arithmetic safeguard; it does **not** certify the caller-declared volume source, classification, or dataset identity.
- **Blind midpoint comparator**: if the first touch approaches the midpoint from the expected side, the comparator *hypothetically* enters at the midpoint **within** that touch bar. This is a blind-limit assumption, not an observed broker fill. It is not allowed to use the rejection bar's close to decide whether the blind order would have existed. The `entryObservedAt` field records the **touch bar close**, the earliest moment its completed OHLC can demonstrate a midpoint-range intersection. It is **not** the bar open, an observed intrabar execution timestamp, or evidence that a broker filled the limit order.
- **Wick-filtered comparator**: a qualifying first-touch wick/reclaim is known only at that touch bar's **close**. Its hypothetical entry is the **next contiguous hourly bar's open**. This avoids trading the already-completed rejection candle with hindsight. The volume gate is reported separately; a wick-only comparator may be displayed while strict volume-qualified evidence remains unavailable.
- **Common markout**: for both comparators, measure directional price change to the **close of the third complete hour after first touch** (configurable fixed 1–48 hours), requiring all intervening bars. The fixed end time is identical but entries differ; the two results are not directly equivalent fill mechanics. Markout is NOT a stop/target backtest. No available future means no return. Report 0/5/15 bps **hypothetical** round-trip cost scenarios, not broker spreads.
- **Subnormal midpoint precision (v3)**: compute the midpoint as `gapLow + (gapHigh - gapLow) / 2` for strictly positive ordered endpoints. This avoids rounding each subnormal half separately, which can shift the first-touch threshold by one floating-point unit and misclassify an opening touch as an unobservable gap-through. Such extreme prices are synthetic numerical-integrity fixtures, not real observed market quotes.
- **Numeric representability**: compute the 50% gap midpoint without overflow from adding two finite prices; reject a research window if the midpoint cannot be represented strictly inside the observed gap. If a gross or cost-adjusted return cannot be represented as finite basis points, markout is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than reporting `Infinity`. Suppress **both** matched comparator markouts if either required comparator is unrepresentable, so selective retention cannot bias the comparison. These guards are software-integrity checks, not market-data authenticity checks.

All emitted candidates are `eligibleForLiveTrading:false`. Source identity and strict timestamps are necessary, but **not sufficient** to prove price provenance. Results are pattern/markout observations only; no independent historical performance has been measured in this PR.

## Interpretation and next acceptance gates

Compare blind-limit and wick-filtered results only on **matched first-touch cohorts**, preserving rejected and unobservable touches in the denominator. Report sample size, instrument, exact archive SHA, UTC date coverage, price-side identity, volume provenance, percentage of first touches rejected, missingness and clustered uncertainty. Do not optimize wick, waiting horizon or markout length against the evaluation holdout. Both strategies need separate cost-stress and a realistic executable two-sided quote study, because a BID-only midpoint touch does not guarantee a buy/sell fill and a next-open BID is not an ASK.

The research still lacks an authenticated broker's timestamped BID/ASK observations, instrument point values, financing cutoffs, exchange sessions, high-impact news timestamps, untouched multi-year holdouts and out-of-sample validation. PR #29 already owns purged holdout planning; PR #24 owns 1H sweep confluence. Integrate with those branches **after** review, without duplicating their files.
