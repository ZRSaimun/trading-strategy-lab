# Leave-one-source-out confluence fragility (offline research only)

`auditConfluenceSourceInfluence(reports)` compares independently validated, frozen confluence OHLC risk-path reports for **one instrument and exactly identical strategy and cost settings**. It checks each source's stop/target, chronology, cost/R arithmetic and summary means before pooling. Duplicate dataset IDs and overlapping simulated trade intervals across archives fail closed. Reports are not merged into a market data feed or refitted.

For each predeclared flat round-trip cost, the output includes trade-weighted pooled mean net bps/R, equal-active-source mean net R, largest source share, and the lowest/highest trade-weighted mean R after removing exactly one source. The most influential source is selected deterministically (lexicographically earliest dataset ID on ties). Sign reversals indicate fragility, not trading signals. Leave-one-source-out estimates are withheld unless there are **at least 30 paths, 12 distinct UTC entry weeks, and 5 nonempty source reports**. These thresholds are disclosure gates, not a statistical power analysis.

A source ID is not an independently authenticated archive identity. Even with nonoverlapping *selected trade* intervals, underlying archive coverage or providers may overlap. The results are descriptive, retrospective, subject to selection bias, and are **not** a genuinely untouched chronological holdout, causal inference, multiple-testing correction, executable CFD quote, financing estimate, or evidence of profit. Never optimize the strategy from leave-one-source-out results. No broker integration, UI, order placement, provider call, secrets or licensed archive content.

Synthetic regression command: `cd frontend && node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/sweep-risk-path-source-influence.test.ts`. Full frontend: `npm test && npm run typecheck && npm run build`. Also require backend, Docker and cloud smoke checks before release. Production deployment requires separately verified Render workspace, backup, rollback and health gates.
