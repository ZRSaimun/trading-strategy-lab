# Offline broker snapshot reconciliation guard — Loop 4

**Status: disconnected, synthetic, read-only, fail-closed.** `backend/app/paper_broker_snapshot_guard.py` accepts a strict owner-declared expected paper ledger and an untrusted observed broker-like snapshot. It compares exact account/provider/venue identities, position side/quantity and quote sets, strictly increasing snapshot sequence/time, bid/ask spread, quote/transport freshness, stop breaches, cash-account notional-versus-equity assumption (not verified buying power), margin, gross leverage, per-position stressed loss and projected daily downside. Amounts use bounded Decimal strings; invalid/unknown fields fail closed.

`CONSISTENT_UNVERIFIED` means only that supplied data are internally consistent. **It is never authentication, risk reservation, a broker-connected protective stop, approval or permission to place even a paper order.** All order flags remain false. The SHA-256 diagnostic digest is neither a signature nor evidence of owner consent. The previous snapshot marker must be stored and updated transactionally by a future trusted caller; a supplied marker is not durable replay protection. This component performs no I/O, no credentials, no journal writes, no broker requests and no actual fill simulation.

A quote is quarantined when its receipt time is later than the snapshot receipt time, or its observation time is not strictly newer than the externally persisted previous snapshot observation time. This conservative causal check prevents a new snapshot identifier from laundering an old quote into apparent readiness. An unchanged quote may therefore hold a valid but quiet market snapshot; it is safer to require manual reconciliation than to treat it as an executable price. This is NOT a durable quote-ID replay registry or independent proof of quote origin.

A long position is marked on BID and a short on ASK. A breached stop halts reconciliation; no stop-price fill is assumed. If the executable BID for a long or ASK for a short is already worse than the declared stressed exit, the reported stress loss uses that worse current side; this also feeds the per-position cap and projected daily downside. Gross notional uses the greater of the entry price and current ASK, so an adverse price decline cannot erase entry-based exposure. These are conservative diagnostic scenarios, not authenticated broker fills, account margin, or maximum-loss guarantees. Stressed exits are owner assumptions; financing, slippage, currency conversion, broker liquidation and outages can cause larger losses. The quote source and point value are not authenticated. No authoritative broker quote or position data are available in this component.

Trading 212's documented public API applies to Invest and Stocks ISA, not CFD accounts: https://docs.trading212.com/api. Even Invest/ISA are **not connected**. Any future eligible live order must have independent secure credentials, a separately verified account, a durable risk reservation, a fresh authenticated quote, explicit owner confirmation for that specific order, non-idempotent retry quarantine and a reconciled broker response. None of these exist here.
