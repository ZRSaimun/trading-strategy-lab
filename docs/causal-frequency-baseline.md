# Loop 5: causal multi-horizon frequency baseline (offline only)

`frontend/lib/causal-frequency-baseline.ts` implements a deliberately simple, reproducible *unconditional* directional-probability research baseline. For 1, 3 and 12 completed-candle horizons, it counts the direction of the most recent disjoint h-candle outcomes and uses Jeffreys Beta(1/2, 1/2) smoothing: `(up + 0.5) / (samples + 1)`. Defaults: 20 minimum and 60 maximum disjoint intervals **per horizon**. Insufficient history yields `INSUFFICIENT` and `probabilityUp: null`, not a made-up probability or trading signal. Disjoint outcome windows reduce mechanical overlap but do not eliminate market serial dependence or regime shifts.

Inputs must be contiguous, aligned, positive-price completed candles with identical instrument/provider/venue/source ID/price side/timeframe. Caller-supplied receipt timestamps must be monotonic, no earlier than candle close and within a bounded receipt lag; issuance must follow the last receipt, occur before the next completed candle close, and be fresh. Mixed sources, gaps, revised/duplicate bars, future observations, delayed historic backfill and retroactive issuance fail closed. This is a causal validation contract, **not** source authentication: receipt timestamps and identity can be forged by an untrusted caller. Do not treat synthetic fixtures or imported historical BID data as a real prospective observation feed.

All output is `UNVERIFIED_LOCAL_RESEARCH`, `live=false`, `execution=NONE`, `quoteTimestamp=null`. Estimates are uncalibrated empirical baselines, not a learned predictive advantage, executable prices, signal, alert, fill or return. They cannot be compared fairly to other models until genuine prospective outcomes are recorded with source-authenticated receipt times, model version, fixed sample-selection policy, proper scoring, costs and untouched evaluation periods. The estimate is not a credible interval or a guarantee of coverage.

Verification: `cd frontend && node --experimental-strip-types --test tests/causal-frequency-baseline.test.ts`. Ten synthetic regression cases cover horizons, abstention, lookahead, gap/source/price validation, receipt timing, bounded samples and determinism. No UI, database, broker, notifications, secrets, account permissions or production datasets are modified.

**Loop 1 handoff:** Provide authorized authenticated completed-candle feed, immutable server receipt timestamps, session-aware gap policy and transactional append-only forecast issuance/settlement. A trusted service must distinguish session closures from missing observations and test crash/replay, concurrent workers and feed provenance. Only after independent forward validation should any model be evaluated for paper trading; a current broker-specific executable bid/ask and owner-confirmed order remain mandatory for real execution.
