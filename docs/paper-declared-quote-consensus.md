# Disconnected multi-feed quote sanity review

`app.paper_declared_quote_consensus.inspect_declared_quote_consensus` accepts **2–5 caller-declared** quotes for one instrument and checks exact schemas, bounded numeric policies, unique source labels, primary-source presence, positive and uncrossed bid/ask, age, transport lag, inter-source observation skew, individual spreads, cross-source mid-price divergence, and inverted bid/ask markets across feeds. Invalid input raises `ValueError`; observed disagreements return `HOLD_QUOTE_REVIEW`.

Even a consistent result is `DECLARED_QUOTE_CONSENSUS_UNVERIFIED`. It does **not** prove sources are independent, authenticate prices, identify real brokerage prices, reserve risk, permit demo/live orders or establish broker API compatibility. Source labels and timestamps can be fabricated or stale; two feeds may derive from the same upstream source. Cross-feed market inversion can be legitimate in asynchronous markets; this deliberately holds for review rather than declaring arbitrage. All authorization flags are false. This is an offline, read-only diagnostic with no HTTP, credentials, persistence or trading side effects.

A production gate must obtain independently authenticated provider quotes, check entitlement/licensing and instrument specifications, bind the exact quote and account snapshots to a durable per-order intent, verify age immediately before submission, re-evaluate after reconnect and fail closed on unavailable feeds. Integrate with atomic risk reservations and per-order owner approval; do not use this diagnostic alone as a trade permission. No guarantee of stop execution, maximum loss or profitability.
