# Offline development source-disjointness audit (Loop 3)

`backend/app/development_source_disjoint_audit.py` is a **pure, read-only metadata validator**, not a market-data importer, backtest engine, or broker adapter. It accepts exact-schema, caller-declared source/venue/instrument/quote-side/account/currency/UTC identity, distinct archive byte digests and time ranges, a declared observation census and expanding development-only folds. It does not accept outcomes or holdout rows.

It rejects reused raw row hashes or source timestamps (including across archive files), duplicate observation IDs, overlapping archive ranges, missing observations, undeclared exclusions, repeated test memberships, non-expanding training, overlapping test windows, missing embargo, train labels unavailable at test time, out-of-window observations, noncausal feature/label clocks, unregistered first tests and extra fields that could carry holdout returns. A later fold may train on an earlier fold's test observations only after labels and embargo are satisfied. A canonical SHA-256 digest binds the supplied declarations, independent of archive/observation/ID-list ordering. At least three distinct UTC test years and three folds are needed for the `DECLARED_TIMING_ONLY` status; otherwise the status is withheld.

The strict one-row-per-observation assumption deliberately rejects some legitimate repeated-feature or multi-horizon uses; those require a separate, independently justified model. Market closures and feed gaps are not inferred from archive intervals. A matching digest does **not** prove bytes, original timestamps, source licenses, complete observation enumeration, pre-outcome registration, real financing, spreads, slippage, broker fills, statistical significance or profitability. A malicious researcher can regenerate all declarations after observing results. Do not read validation/holdout outcomes to select candidates; independently freeze and anchor the plan and archive digests before evaluation, then enforce access control and audit logs outside this helper.

Synthetic tests: `cd backend && PYTHONPATH=. python -m pytest -q tests/test_development_source_disjoint_audit.py`. No network, credentials, order routes, data mutation or execution permissions are introduced. Do not merge or deploy without exact-head full CI, verified production target, tested restore/rollback and independent health checks.
